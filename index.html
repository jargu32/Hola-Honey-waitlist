Honey with a Higher Purpose

Rooted in Central America. Powered by opportunity.

Our story begins in the high-altitude mountain forests of La Virtud, Honduras, where local beekeepers care for bees surrounded by coffee blossoms, citrus trees, and pine canopy.

Hola Honey was born out of a deep commitment to People Over Profit. We work directly with smallholder Honduran beekeeping families, providing fair wages, sustainable hive equipment, and re-investing profits into rural community education and bee habitat protection.

THE HONEY JOURNEY

From Hive to Jar

01

Mountain Foraging

Bees forage freely in pristine tropical mountain forests away from synthetic pesticides or industrial farms.

02

Ethical Harvesting

Our beekeepers harvest with care, leaving sufficient honey stores to support the health of each colony.

03

Cold Extraction

Honey is gently spun out of combs without heat processing, preserving all natural pollen, enzymes, and medicinal properties.

04

Artisanal Jarring

Carefully bottled in glass jars and finished with attention to every detail.
